📖 About Us

Hello! Welcome to the Aemy Collective! (If you know you know ^^), A solo-startup project by a Wuthering Waves Enthusiast. If you ever come into contact with me, you can call me Jun.

💙 About me
As described above, I'm a big fan of the whole Wuthering Waves franchise and a newbie to the TCG play scene. I'm hoping to invite interested rovers to get into the TCG as well and share the same enthusiasm as me! My vision for this project is to appeal to new players who find the whole TCG scene daunting as I did myself, rather than focusing on the inevitable monetary value of the cards themselves, similar to the game, you should be able to obtain whoever you like! In this case, whichever character's playstyle you like!

Some things you could look forward to for this project/website is being able to view full playsets for each character that'll be purchaseable, Fully customizable decks to your liking to purchase, a section to view community decks and pre-made decks that have won in tournaments as well as someone to share your excitement about the game with!

⚠️ DISCLAIMER: I'm in no way affiliated with Kuro Games, Unicorn Cards or Wuthering Waves. This is purely a passion project and an ambitious project to connect this small but growing community! Hope to meet more rovers! ^^
